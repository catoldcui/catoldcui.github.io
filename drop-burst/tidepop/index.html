The sea has stopped.

Tap one drop. It fills, it bursts, and it sprays water four ways to the nearest drop in each direction, so those burst too. Pop, pop, pop: one tap can chain across the whole board.

  • One tap, one huge chain. Every drop in the chain adds score and stacks the multiplier. The right tap beats a hundred random ones.
  • A new build every dive. Pick one of 4 divers, mix 6 weapons, 34 relics and 5 cursed treasures, cross three seas, beat three bosses, then dive deeper through Depths 1–10.
  • A new sea every day. The Daily Sea gives every player the same board. Share a seed or challenge link and race a friend on the exact same sea.

Chapter 1 is free to play in full. One purchase unlocks the full game. No ads. No subscriptions.
