  • Full Version (one-time purchase): a non-consumable item. The free part includes the first chapter at depth 0 (played with the "Gunner" diver only), a single free try of the "Daily Sea" (one in total, not one per day) and opening challenge links (these also start at depth 0 and can be played to the end of the first chapter); the Full Version unlocks the remaining chapters, content from depth 1 onward, the "Daily Sea" beyond that one free try (including the current day's), past daily challenges, seed entry, and divers other than the Gunner (these divers still have to be unlocked in the Game with Pearls). With or without the Full Version, the "Daily Sea" (including the free try) opens only after you finish your first dive in a save; a dive counts as finished whether you win, lose or give it up.

Prices are as shown on the App Store. Gem packs are only available to players who own the Full Version. Gems are used in the "Daily Sea": the first try of the current day's Daily Sea each day is free, and each further try that day uses 1 gem. Past daily challenges and seed entry never use gems, and gems cannot be used without the Full Version. If the Full Version is refunded and revoked, gems already in your save stay there but cannot be used, and they are not refunded separately; if you get the Full Version again, they can be used again (the 10 bonus gems are not credited a second time for the same save). Refunds of gem packs themselves are handled by Apple (see "Refunds" below). Gems are stored only in the Game's local save on your device; "Reset All Data" in Settings keeps them, but deleting the Game removes them (unless the save is restored from a device backup), and because Apple does not restore consumable purchases, "Restore Purchases" does not bring gems back. Gems cannot be shared through Family Sharing.
